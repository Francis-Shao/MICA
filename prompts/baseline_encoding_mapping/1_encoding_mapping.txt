Accident Report:
{report}

Task:
Encode the pre-crash scenario of the two vehicles involved in the final collision.

Important Definitions:
- Only focus on the two vehicles involved in the final collision.
- V1 and V2 must refer to the two collision-involved vehicles.
- Prefer assigning V1 to the striking vehicle, the vehicle making the main maneuver,
  or the vehicle whose action most directly creates the conflict.
- Assign V2 to the struck vehicle or the counterpart vehicle in the conflict.
- If the striking vehicle is unclear, assign V1 and V2 according to their order of
  appearance in the collision description.
- Encode only the pre-crash state immediately relevant to the collision.
- Each field must contain exactly one categorical value.
- Do not extract a complete behavior timeline.
- Do not infer information after the collision.
- If a field is ambiguous, choose the best supported value from the report.

Encoding Fields:

Crash Type:
1 = Head-on
2 = Side swipe
3 = Rear end
4 = Broadside
5 = Other

Direction V1 / V2:
0 = Stopped
1 = Forward
2 = Backward

Speed Change V1 / V2:
0 = Constant
1 = Accelerating
2 = Decelerating

Relative Position:
1 = Same lane & direction
2 = Same lane reverse direction
3 = Lateral lane - right
4 = Lateral lane - left

Movement - Turning V1 / V2:
0 = None
1 = Right turn
2 = Left turn

Movement - Other V1 / V2:
1 = Proceeding straight
2 = Passing other vehicle
3 = Changing lanes
4 = Making U turn
5 = Entering traffic
6 = Merging
7 = Ran off road
8 = Parking maneuver
9 = Parked
10 = Travelling wrong way
11 = Lane splitting
12 = Other
13 = None

Output Format:
Return only valid JSON in the following format.

{{
  "vehicles": {{
    "V1": "vehicle name",
    "V2": "vehicle name"
  }},
  "encoding": {{
    "crash_type": integer,
    "direction_v1": integer,
    "direction_v2": integer,
    "speed_change_v1": integer,
    "speed_change_v2": integer,
    "relative_position": integer,
    "movement_turning_v1": integer,
    "movement_turning_v2": integer,
    "movement_other_v1": integer,
    "movement_other_v2": integer
  }}
}}

Remember:
- Output JSON only.
- Do not include markdown.
- Do not include explanations.
